# Factors Influencing Geographic Mobility in the United States

## Event-Driven Migration Factors

### Pandemic Impact
- COVID-19 triggered significant migration patterns, with people leaving dense urban 
centers for suburban and rural areas to avoid high infection rates and take advantage 
of remote work flexibility
- Many workers relocated from high-cost cities when no longer tethered to physical offices
- Some families moved closer to extended family for support systems during lockdowns

### Natural Disasters
- Hurricane displacement (Katrina, Maria, Ian) forcing permanent or temporary relocation
- Increasing wildfire frequency in Western states driving populations from high-risk regions
- Flooding in coastal areas and river basins leading to managed retreat
- Drought conditions affecting agricultural communities and water-stressed regions

### Economic Disruptions
- Factory closures and industrial decline creating "rust belt" outmigration
- Tech industry layoffs influencing movement from technology hubs
- Housing market crashes leading to foreclosure-driven migration
- Regional economic recessions causing labor market relocations

## Underlying Structural Mobility Factors

### Economic Opportunity
- Wage differentials between regions
- Job market strength and employment rates
- Industry specialization and career advancement potential
- Lower business operation costs attracting entrepreneurship

### Lifestyle and Quality of Life
- Housing affordability and cost of living disparities
- Education quality and access to higher education
- Healthcare system quality and accessibility
- Climate preferences and seasonal considerations
- Urban amenities versus rural space and privacy
- Proximity to recreational opportunities

### Demographic and Life Stage Factors
- Retirement migration patterns (typically to warmer climates and lower-tax states)
- Family formation and relationship changes
- Educational transitions (college entry and graduation)
- Empty nest downsizing
- Aging-related needs and healthcare access

### Cultural and Social Considerations
- Political alignment with local governance
- Religious community presence
- Cultural amenities and diversity
- Social network distribution
- Community identity and belonging
